## How the Channels interact

The pedal has two drive Channels. **Channel 1** is the overdrive side, voiced by **OD Type**. **Channel 2** is the amp side, voiced by **Amp Type**. Each has its own Gain, Vol and Tone knobs and its own footswitch. Three settings decide how the two work together.

### Effect Order: which Channel comes first

**Effect Order** is the middle toggle. It sets how the Channels combine when both are on:

- **2>1**: Channel 2 feeds Channel 1, so the amp side runs into the overdrive side.
- **2+1**: the Channels run in parallel, side by side.
- **2<1**: Channel 1 feeds Channel 2, so the overdrive side pushes into the amp side.

### Clean 1: a clean boost or a clean blend

The **Clean 1** dip removes most of the clipping from Channel 1, so it becomes a clean boost rather than an overdrive. Each OD Type keeps its own character and frequency response, so 808, Super and RJM still sound different.

Where that boost sits depends on Effect Order. At 2<1 it boosts into Channel 2, and at 2>1 it comes after it. At **2+1** it runs beside Channel 2, so Channel 1 becomes a **clean blend**: clean signal alongside the drive.

### Master: one volume for the whole pedal

Normally each Channel's Vol knob sets that Channel's level. The **Master** dip changes this:

- **Vol 2** sets the overall output volume at all times, even with Channel 2 bypassed.
- **Vol 1** has no effect unless both Channels are on. With both on, it becomes a **stack mix** control whose interaction depends on Effect Order.

With Master on, set the loudness with Vol 2, and use Vol 1 to shape the mix when both Channels are on.

### Reading the help cards

The help card for each control follows these settings. Vol 1 says when Master leaves it with no effect or makes it a stack mix control, Clean 1 says when it is a clean blend, and Contour 2 and Presence 2 describe what they do for the current Amp Type.
